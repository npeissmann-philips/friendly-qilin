# Credit Concession Policy - 2025

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Confidentiality Level:** Internal (Suitable for RAG Study Project - Phase 2)

---

## 1. Introduction and Strategic Objectives

This document outlines the **2025 Credit Concession Policy**. The primary goal for this fiscal year is rapid customer acquisition and market penetration following the economic recovery phase.

**Key Strategic Objectives for 2025:**
1. **Portfolio Growth:** Increase the overall credit portfolio volume by 15%.
2. **Risk Tolerance:** Maintain Non-Performing Loans (NPL) under 4.5% across all portfolios.
3. **Digital Onboarding:** Transition 80% of new loan originations to the mobile application.

---

## 2. Customer Segmentation and Credit Lines

The concession policy is divided into three primary segments.

### 2.1. Retail (Individuals / Pessoa Física)
*   **Target Audience:** Individuals seeking personal loans and auto loans.
*   **Key Products:**
    *   *Standard Personal Loan:* Up to $10,000 (Requires proof of income).
    *   *Auto Finance:* Up to 90% of vehicle value (Maximum term: 48 months).

### 2.2. Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs / PMEs)
*   **Target Audience:** Businesses with annual revenue between $50,000 and $5,000,000.
*   **Key Products:**
    *   *Working Capital:* Up to 15% of verified annual revenue.
    *   *Invoice Discounting:* Up to 80% advance on approved client invoices.

### 2.3. Corporate (Large Enterprises)
*   **Target Audience:** Businesses with annual revenue exceeding $5,000,000.
*   **Key Products:**
    *   *Syndicated Loans:* Evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

---

## 3. Risk Assessment and Credit Scoring Framework

For 2025, the primary underwriting metric remains the traditional credit bureau score, with pilot programs testing alternative data.

### 3.1. Underwriting Criteria
*   **Minimum Credit Score:** 650 (Traditional Bureau Score).
*   **Debt-to-Income (DTI) Limit:**
    *   Retail: Maximum 40% of gross monthly income.
    *   SMEs: Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) must be greater than 1.15x.
*   **Open Finance Requirement:** Open Finance data sharing is purely optional and can be used to manually appeal a rejected application.

---

## 4. Interest Rates and Pricing Models

Pricing is tied to the Central Bank's base rate, with fixed spreads based on the credit score tier.

*   **Base Rate:** Central Bank Rate + Spread.
*   **Risk Premium:** 
    *   Tier 1 (Score > 750): +1.0%
    *   Tier 2 (Score 700-749): +2.5%
    *   Tier 3 (Score 650-699): +4.5%
*   **Promotional Discount:** -0.5% discount for customers who set up automatic payroll deductions (Consignado).

---

## 5. Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Directives

While ESG is a growing concern, the 2025 policy focuses primarily on baseline regulatory compliance rather than proactive credit incentives.

*   **Exclusion List:**
    *   Companies currently under federal investigation for environmental crimes.
    *   Arms manufacturing without proper government licensing.

---

## 6. Approval Workflows and Automation

*   **Level 1 - Fully Automated:**
    *   Criteria: Score > 700, DTI < 30%, Loan Amount < $10,000.
    *   SLA: 2 hours.
*   **Level 2 - Manual Underwriting:**
    *   Criteria: All SME and Corporate loans, or Retail loans not meeting Level 1 criteria.
    *   SLA: 2-3 business days.

---

## 7. Fraud Prevention and Compliance

*   **Data Retention:** All application data is kept for 7 years as per standard financial regulations.
*   **Anti-Fraud Measures:** Mandatory two-factor authentication (2FA) via SMS for all loan disbursements.
