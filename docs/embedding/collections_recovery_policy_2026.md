## 1. Introduction and Core Philosophy

This document outlines the **2026 Collections and Debt Recovery Policy**. Following the aggressive credit expansion outlined in the 2026 Credit Concession Policy, our collections strategy is focused on maximizing recovery rates while maintaining strict adherence to consumer protection laws and preserving customer relationships whenever possible.

**Key Objectives for 2026:**
1. **Early Intervention:** Reduce the roll-rate from 30-day delinquency to 60-day delinquency by 12%.
2. **AI-Driven Communication:** Utilize predictive modeling to determine the optimal channel (SMS, Email, WhatsApp, Phone) and time for contacting delinquent customers.
3. **Restructuring:** Prioritize loan restructuring over legal action for customers demonstrating genuine financial hardship.

## 2. Delinquency Stages and Actions (Retail & SME)

The recovery process is segmented based on the number of Days Past Due (DPD).

### 2.1. Pre-Collections (1 to 15 DPD)
*   **Status:** "Grace Period / Oversight"
*   **Actions:**
    *   Automated gentle reminders via SMS and Push Notification on DPD 3.
    *   No penalty interest applied if the debt is settled within 5 days of the due date.
    *   No reporting to external credit bureaus.

### 2.2. Soft Collections (16 to 60 DPD)
*   **Status:** "Past Due"
*   **Actions:**
    *   Multi-channel outreach including WhatsApp and personalized emails.
    *   Penalty interest of 2% + 1% pro-rata per month is applied.
    *   Account is officially reported to credit bureaus (Serasa/SPC) on DPD 30.
    *   Offer of standard refinancing with a maximum term extension of 12 months.

### 2.3. Hard Collections (61 to 120 DPD)
*   **Status:** "Default Risk"
*   **Actions:**
    *   Outbound calling by human agents.
    *   Temporary suspension of all active credit lines and credit cards associated with the client.
    *   Introduction of aggressive settlement discounts (up to 30% waiver on accrued interest).

### 2.4. Late Stage & Legal (121+ DPD)
*   **Status:** "Non-Performing Loan (NPL)"
*   **Actions:**
    *   Debt is transferred to external collection agencies or sold to distressed asset buyers at a discount.
    *   For auto loans and mortgages: Initiation of asset repossession or foreclosure proceedings.
    *   For unsecured debts above $25,000: Assessment for civil litigation.

## 3. Hardship and Restructuring Programs

Customers facing verifiable financial distress (e.g., job loss, severe medical emergency, natural disaster) are eligible for the **2026 Compassionate Restructuring Program**.

*   **Eligibility Criteria:** Must provide documented proof of hardship. Customer must not have missed a payment in the 6 months prior to the hardship event.
*   **Benefits:**
    *   Up to 90 days of payment holiday (interest continues to accrue but is deferred).
    *   Waiver of all late fees and penalty interest.
    *   Restructuring of the principal balance over a new term up to 72 months at a reduced "Cost of Funds" interest rate.

---

## 4. Collections Agent Guidelines & Compliance

All internal and external collection agents must strictly adhere to the following compliance rules to avoid regulatory fines:

*   **Contact Hours:** Customers may only be contacted between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM on weekdays, and 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM on Saturdays. No contact allowed on Sundays or national holidays.
*   **Workplace Contact:** Calling a customer at their place of employment is strictly prohibited unless the customer specifically requests it as their preferred contact method.
*   **Third-Party Disclosure:** Agents must never disclose the existence of a debt to a customer's family members, colleagues, or neighbors.
