# Credit Concession Policy - 2026

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Confidentiality Level:** Internal (Suitable for RAG Study Project - Phase 2)

---

## 1. Introduction and Strategic Objectives

This document outlines the **2026 Credit Concession Policy**. The primary goal for this fiscal year is to balance aggressive market expansion with rigorous risk management, heavily leveraging Open Finance data and Artificial Intelligence (AI) for real-time decision-making.

**Key Strategic Objectives for 2026:**
1. **Risk Mitigation:** Maintain Non-Performing Loans (NPL) under 3.5% across all portfolios.
2. **Financial Inclusion:** Increase credit access for underserved demographics utilizing alternative data scoring.
3. **ESG Integration:** Prioritize funding for sustainable projects and penalize high-carbon footprint initiatives.

---

## 2. Customer Segmentation and Credit Lines

The concession policy is divided into three primary segments, each with specific criteria and product offerings.

### 2.1. Retail (Individuals / Pessoa Física)
*   **Target Audience:** Individuals seeking personal loans, auto loans, and mortgages.
*   **Key Products:**
    *   *Microcredit Advance:* Up to $5,000 (No collateral required).
    *   *Auto Finance:* Up to 80% of vehicle value (Maximum term: 60 months).
    *   *Mortgage (Green Home):* Special rates for energy-efficient homes.

### 2.2. Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs / PMEs)
*   **Target Audience:** Businesses with annual revenue between $100,000 and $10,000,000.
*   **Key Products:**
    *   *Working Capital:* Up to 25% of verified annual revenue.
    *   *Equipment Finance:* Tied to ESG compliance (e.g., solar panels, electric fleets).

### 2.3. Corporate (Large Enterprises)
*   **Target Audience:** Businesses with annual revenue exceeding $10,000,000.
*   **Key Products:**
    *   *Syndicated Loans:* Evaluated on a case-by-case basis.
    *   *Revolving Credit Facilities:* Requires quarterly financial auditing.

## 3. Risk Assessment and Credit Scoring Framework

For 2026, the traditional credit bureau score is augmented by an internal **AI-Driven Behavioral Score (AI-BS)**.

### 3.1. Underwriting Criteria
*   **Minimum Credit Score:** 620 (Traditional) OR an AI-BS in the top 40th percentile.
*   **Debt-to-Income (DTI) Limit:**
    *   Retail: Maximum 35% of gross monthly income.
    *   SMEs: Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) must be greater than 1.25x.
*   **Open Finance Requirement:** For limits exceeding $20,000 (Retail) or $100,000 (SME), the customer MUST consent to 12 months of Open Finance data sharing.

### 3.2. Alternative Data Utilization
*   Utility bill payment history.
*   Transactional cash flow consistency.
*   Digital footprint and behavioral biometrics during the application process.

## 4. Interest Rates and Pricing Models

Pricing is dynamic and tied to the Central Bank's base rate, adjusted for individual risk profiles and ESG factors.

*   **Base Rate:** Central Bank Rate + Spread.
*   **Risk Premium:** +0.5% to +4.0% depending on the AI-BS tier.
*   **ESG Discount (The "Green Modifier"):**
    *   Retail: -0.5% for purchasing electric vehicles.
    *   SMEs/Corporate: -1.0% if the company proves a 20% reduction in carbon emissions year-over-year.

---

## 5. Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Directives

By Q3 2026, no credit above $500,000 will be approved without an ESG compliance check.

*   **Exclusion List (Zero Credit Policy):**
    *   Uncertified deforestation activities.
    *   Fossil fuel extraction without an active transition plan.
    *   Companies with documented labor rights violations in the last 24 months.

---

## 6. Approval Workflows and Automation

To increase operational efficiency, the 2026 policy dictates the following approval pathways:

*   **Level 1 - Fully Automated (Straight-Through Processing - STP):**
    *   Criteria: Score > 750, DTI < 25%, Loan Amount < $15,000.
    *   SLA: Instant approval.
*   **Level 2 - Hybrid (AI Analysis + Junior Analyst Review):**
    *   Criteria: Score 620-749, Loan Amount $15,000 - $100,000, or minor data anomalies.
    *   SLA: 4 hours.
*   **Level 3 - Committee Review (Manual Underwriting):**
    *   Criteria: Corporate loans, ESG exceptions, or amounts > $100,000.
    *   SLA: 3-5 business days.

---

## 7. Fraud Prevention and Compliance (LGPD/GDPR)

*   **Data Retention:** Rejected application data must be anonymized within 90 days. Approved application data is kept for 5 years post-loan closure.
*   **Anti-Fraud Measures:** Mandatory liveness check (facial recognition) for mobile originations. Device fingerprinting must match the declared geographical residence.
